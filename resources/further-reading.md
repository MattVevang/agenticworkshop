# 📚 Further Reading & Resources

Want to keep learning after the workshop? Here are some of the best free resources out there.

---

## 🛠️ Tools We Used

| Resource | Link | What It Is |
|----------|------|------------|
| **Ollama** | [ollama.com](https://ollama.com) | Run open-source LLMs locally on your own machine — the engine behind our workshop. |
| **Open WebUI** | [docs.openwebui.com](https://docs.openwebui.com) | The chat interface we used to talk to our models — open source and self-hosted. |
| **GitHub Copilot** | [docs.github.com/copilot](https://docs.github.com/en/copilot) | AI pair programmer that helps you write code faster — free for students with GitHub Education. |

---

## 🎓 Learn How AI Actually Works

| Resource | Link | What It Is |
|----------|------|------------|
| **3Blue1Brown — "But what is a neural network?"** | [youtube.com/playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi) | The best visual explanation of neural networks on the internet — beautiful animations, zero gatekeeping. |
| **Andrej Karpathy — "Intro to Large Language Models"** | [youtube.com/watch?v=zjkBMFhNj_g](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjkBMFhNj_g) | A 1-hour talk from an ex-OpenAI researcher that explains LLMs clearly — great starting point for understanding how ChatGPT-style models work. |
| **Hugging Face** | [huggingface.co](https://huggingface.co) | The biggest open hub for AI models, datasets, and free courses — like GitHub but for machine learning. |

---

## 🏆 Compare & Explore Models

| Resource | Link | What It Is |
|----------|------|------------|
| **LMSys Chatbot Arena** | [lmarena.ai](https://lmarena.ai/) | Compare AI models head-to-head in blind tests — you pick the winner and the community ranks them. |
| **Ollama Model Library** | [ollama.com/library](https://ollama.com/library) | Browse all the models you can run locally with Ollama — filter by size, capability, and popularity. |

## ✍️ Get Better at Prompting

| Resource | Link | What It Is |
|----------|------|------------|
| **Prompt Engineering Guide** | [promptingguide.ai](https://www.promptingguide.ai/) | A comprehensive, community-maintained guide to writing better prompts — from basics to advanced techniques. |
| **Learn Prompting** | [learnprompting.org](https://learnprompting.org/) | Free interactive course on prompt engineering — great for beginners who want structured lessons. |

## 🛡️ AI Safety & Ethics

| Resource | Link | What It Is |
|----------|------|------------|
| **AI Safety Fundamentals** | [aisafetyfundamentals.com](https://aisafetyfundamentals.com/) | Free courses on AI alignment and safety — understand why building safe AI matters. |
