# 📰 AI News of the Week

A drop-in template for presenters to share 2–3 current AI news stories at the start of the workshop. Keeps the content feeling fresh and relevant.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. **The week before your workshop**, scan the sources below for 2–3 interesting stories
2. **Fill in the template** below (or add a slide to the PowerPoint deck)
3. **Spend 3–5 minutes** at the start of the workshop sharing these stories
4. **Ask students**: "Has anyone heard about this?" — it's a great icebreaker

## 💡 Tips for Picking Good Stories

- **Relatable**: Pick stories students might encounter (new chatbot features, AI in social media, AI in gaming)
- **Balanced**: Mix positive stories (AI helping medicine) with cautionary ones (deepfake concerns)
- **Recent**: Within the last 1–2 weeks if possible
- **Discussion-ready**: Stories that raise questions ("Is this cool or creepy?") work best
- **Avoid**: Highly technical papers, funding announcements, or stories that require deep domain knowledge

### Story Ideas That Always Work

- New model releases (GPT-X, Gemini, Llama, etc.)
- AI-generated content controversies (art, music, writing)
- AI in school policies and education
- Cool demos or apps students can try
- AI fails and hallucinations in the news
- New regulations or laws about AI
