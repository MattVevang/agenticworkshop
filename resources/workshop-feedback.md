# 📊 Workshop Feedback & Polling

Collecting feedback helps improve the workshop for future sessions. Here's how to set up a quick, easy feedback mechanism.

---

## 🎯 Quick Setup: Google Forms Survey

The fastest approach is a short Google Form. Create one at [forms.google.com](https://forms.google.com) and generate a QR code.

### Recommended Survey Questions (5 Questions, ~2 Minutes)

| # | Question | Type |
|---|----------|------|
| 1 | **What was your favorite lab?** | Multiple choice: Lab 1–9 |
| 2 | **What was the most surprising thing you learned?** | Short answer |
| 3 | **How confident do you feel using AI tools now?** (1 = not at all, 5 = very confident) | Linear scale 1–5 |
| 4 | **Would you recommend this workshop to a friend?** | Yes / No / Maybe |
| 5 | **What would you add or change?** | Short answer (optional) |

---

## 📱 QR Code Generation

Generate a QR code for your feedback form:

1. **Google Forms built-in**: Click the **Send** button → click the link icon → shorten URL → use any QR generator
2. **Free QR generators**: [qr-code-generator.com](https://www.qr-code-generator.com/), [goqr.me](https://goqr.me/), or simply search "QR code generator"
3. **Add to last slide**: Include the QR code on the final discussion/Q&A slide so students can scan on their way out

> **💡 Tip:** Print a few copies of the QR code and place them around the room so students can scan easily.

## 🖥️ Live Polling (Optional)

If you want real-time interaction during the workshop, consider these free tools:

| Tool | Link | Best For |
|------|------|----------|
| **Mentimeter** | [mentimeter.com](https://www.mentimeter.com/) | Word clouds, live polls, quizzes |
| **Slido** | [slido.com](https://www.slido.com/) | Q&A and polls (free for up to 100 participants) |
| **Poll Everywhere** | [polleverywhere.com](https://www.polleverywhere.com/) | Simple polls with no student login |

### Suggested Live Poll Questions

Use these during the workshop to keep energy up:

- **Opening poll**: "Have you ever used an AI chatbot before?" (Yes / No / Not sure)
- **After Lab 2**: "Which model gave the best answer?" (show model names)
- **After Lab 4**: "Do you trust AI to get facts right?" (Always / Sometimes / Never / Depends)
- **After Lab 7**: "Who should be responsible when AI is biased?" (The company / The user / The government / All of them)
- **Closing poll**: "What will you use AI for first after today?" (Word cloud)

## 📈 What to Do with Feedback

- **Review within a week** while memory is fresh
- **Look for patterns** — if multiple students mention the same thing, prioritize it
- **Share highlights** with school administrators to build support for future workshops
- **Iterate** — use feedback to improve labs, timing, and content for the next session

---

*Good feedback makes the next workshop even better. Two minutes from students today saves hours of guesswork later! 📝*
